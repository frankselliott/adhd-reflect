# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 31 May 2026**

## The short version

ADHD Reflect is built for people in difficult parenting moments. The things you type, tap and answer here are often emotionally sensitive. We treat them that way.

- We collect the minimum we can.
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not use your data to target advertising.
- We do not share what you type into the search with advertisers or anyone marketing to you.
- We use a small number of trusted services to run the site, send emails and match your input to the right card or guide.
- You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

If anything here is unclear, email **privacy@adhdreflect.com** and we will answer in plain English.

---

## Who runs ADHD Reflect

ADHD Reflect is operated by **ADHD Reflect**. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean ADHD Reflect. "You" means anyone using the site, the search, the quiz, the newsletter or any of our features.

We respect privacy laws in all jurisdictions where our users live, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), GDPR, and other applicable data protection regulations. We apply the strictest reasonable standard across the board rather than running different versions for different regions.

## What we collect

### Things you give us directly

**Email address.** If you sign up for the practice plan, create an account or save your quiz result, we collect your email address.

**Quiz answers.** If you take the parenting pattern quiz, we collect your answers and the pattern result. If you create an account, we store this so we can match weekly practices to your pattern. If you do not create an account, your answers are processed once and not stored.

**Account details.** If you create an account, we store the email you used to sign in and a hashed version of your password. We do not store your password in readable form.

**What you type into the search.** When you describe what just happened, we receive that text. If you use voice input, your browser converts speech to text on your device and we receive only the text. See "What we do with what you type" below.

**Payment details.** If you ever upgrade to a paid tier, payment is processed by **Stripe**. We do not see or store your card number. We receive a record that you paid and your subscription status.

### Things we collect automatically

**Country-level location.** We use your IP address to determine your country so we can show you the right crisis lines and support resources. We do not store precise location.

**Basic technical information.** Standard web logs including IP address, device type, browser, pages visited and timestamps. Used for security and debugging.

**Analytics.** We use privacy-friendly analytics that count page views without cookies and without identifying individual users.

### Things we do not collect

We do not collect or ask for your child's name, photo, school or identifying details, your partner's details, your address, your phone number, your health records, diagnosis or prescription information, or any details you have not chosen to share.

If you type identifying details into the search, we will receive them. Please do not. The search works with general descriptions, not names or identifying information.

## What we do with what you type

This is the most sensitive part of the site.

When you type something into the search, here is what happens:

1. Your text is sent to **Anthropic's Claude API**. Anthropic uses it only to return a match to one of our cards. Per Anthropic's published terms, API inputs are **not used to train their models**.
2. The search returns a match to the most relevant card.
3. We log the **matched card ID**, the **general category** and an **anonymised version of the query** so we can identify gaps in our card library.
4. Logs are retained for **90 days** then automatically deleted.

We run a crisis-language check on what you type. If you describe something that looks like a safety risk to you, your child or someone else, we show crisis support options instead of a card.

We do not link what you type to your email, account or payment details.

## Sensitive information

ADHD Reflect is not a health service. We do not provide diagnosis, therapy, treatment or clinical care. But we recognise that ADHD-related content, quiz answers and descriptions of parenting moments are emotionally sensitive.

We treat all of this as sensitive by default:

- We only collect it where it is needed to give you what you came for.
- We apply tight access controls inside our systems.
- We do not share it with advertisers or marketers.
- We do not use it to build a profile that follows you around the internet.

## Who we share data with

| Service | What it does | What we share |
|---|---|---|
| **Anthropic (Claude API)** | Matches your search input to the right card. | The text you type. Not used for model training. |
| **Email provider** | Sends practice plan emails. | Email address and quiz pattern. |
| **Stripe** | Processes payments. | Name, email and payment details (handled by Stripe). |
| **Cloudflare** | Hosts the site. | Standard technical logs. |

We do not share what you type into the search with any service other than Anthropic. We do not share your quiz result with anyone other than our email provider.

We will only share your data outside this list if you ask us to, we are required to by law, we need to protect someone's safety, or we transfer the business (in which case we will tell you first).

## Your rights

No matter where you live, you can:

- **Know** what we have about you.
- **Access** a copy of your data.
- **Correct** anything that is wrong.
- **Delete** your data.
- **Opt out** of marketing emails at any time.
- **Complain** to us, and to a regulator if we cannot resolve it.

**California residents:** You have rights under the CCPA and CPRA, including the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as defined under California law.

**EU and UK residents:** You have rights under GDPR. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email **privacy@adhdreflect.com**. We will respond within 30 days.

## Children

ADHD Reflect is for adults, especially parents. It is not designed for or marketed to anyone under 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe your child has used the site and provided information, email **privacy@adhdreflect.com** and we will delete it.

Do not type information that would identify your child into the search or any form on the site. The site works with general descriptions of moments, not identifying details.

## Security

We use industry-standard protections including encryption in transit, hashed passwords and role-based access controls.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever have a data breach likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected users and relevant regulators as required by law.
