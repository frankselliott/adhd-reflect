## The short version

Some of the links on ADHD Reflect are referral links. If you sign up to one of those services through us, we earn a small commission. It does not change the price you pay, and it does not change what we recommend.

---

## What this means

A referral link is a normal link with a code on the end, so the other company knows you came from us. When someone signs up through it, that company pays us a commission.

We only use these where they are clearly labelled. Right now the main one is a link to **online-therapy.com**, which appears in the email you get after buying Both of You. If you sign up to their service through that link, we earn a commission.

---

## What it does not change

- **It does not change your price.** You pay exactly what you would have paid going direct. The commission comes out of the company's side, not yours.
- **It does not change what we recommend.** We link to something because we think it is genuinely useful, not because of the commission. If a paid service stopped being worth recommending, we would drop the link regardless of what it paid.
- **It is not clinical advice.** A referral is a pointer to a service that might help. It is not a diagnosis, a treatment plan, or advice about what is right for your situation. That is between you and a qualified professional.
